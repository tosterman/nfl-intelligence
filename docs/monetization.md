# Commercial activation

The product is an independent analytics publication. Google AdSense/Ad Manager is the relevant publisher path for selling inventory; Google Ads is primarily the advertiser buying interface.

Before accepting revenue:
1. Confirm the operator identity, business contact and production domain.
2. Use a hosting plan whose terms cover commercial use; connected Vercel team was Hobby during initial inspection.
3. Enable and verify Vercel analytics; monitor returning readers, game detail opens, team hub visits, successful forecast refreshes and performance.
4. Apply for Google publisher approval using the actual content and market-language policy. Approval and ad fill are not guaranteed. Gambling-promoting inventory can face restrictions.
5. Configure a certified consent management platform where Google requires one; current analytics preference controls are not such certification.
6. Add the exact approved publisher ID and ads.txt authorization. Start with one clearly labeled in-content slot after the game slate or analysis, never inside a probability or verdict.
7. Monitor revenue per thousand pageviews, viewability, Core Web Vitals, retention and ad-policy notices. Do not optimize predictions to improve ad clicks.

No paid subscription, ad contract, provider contract or recurring charge was authorized or purchased during the build. Licensed timestamped odds/personnel/weather feeds are separate infrastructure decisions. Avoid promises of betting profits or future advertising income.

Local layout evidence: `reviews/ad-layout-feasibility.md` compares reserved and unreserved browser-only placement after the slate. Reserving geometry prevented downstream movement during test fill/removal at three widths in Chromium and WebKit. This informs future integration, but no ad slot or publisher tags are implemented and real-ad/consent verification remains open.

Sources reviewed: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/10437795 and https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/10437963 . Recheck current policies before activation.
